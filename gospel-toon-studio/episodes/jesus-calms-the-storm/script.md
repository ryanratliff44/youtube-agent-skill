# Jesus Sleeps Through the Storm (example episode)

**Passage:** Mark 4:35-41 (parallels: Matthew 8:23-27, Luke 8:22-25)
**Translation quoted:** KJV
**Length:** ~150 words (~60 s)

## Beats
0. Hook: the storm is so violent fishermen think they'll die; Jesus is asleep.
1. Evening; "Let us pass over unto the other side" (Mark 4:35); they set off.
2. The storm; boat filling (4:37); Jesus asleep on a pillow in the stern (4:38).
3. "Master, carest thou not that we perish?" (4:38)
4. "Peace, be still." Great calm (4:39).
5. "Why are ye so fearful?..." (4:40); "What manner of man is this..." (4:41)
6. Reflection + reference.

## Scripture vs. tradition
- "Professional fishermen": Peter, Andrew, James and John were fishermen (Mark 1:16-20); that they
  were in this boat is implied, not stated. Peter and John appear in shots for continuity.
- Night/lightning/moonlight visuals are artistic; Mark says "when the even was come" (4:35).
- The closing reflection is application, not scripture.

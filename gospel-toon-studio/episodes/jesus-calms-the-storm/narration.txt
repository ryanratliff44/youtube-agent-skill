Bro. Professional fishermen thought they were about to die in this storm. And Jesus? Asleep. On a pillow.

Evening on the Sea of Galilee. Jesus says, "Let us pass over unto the other side." So they all hop in a little wooden boat and push off. Easy night. Or so they thought.

Then the wind hits. Waves are crashing over the sides. The boat is literally filling with water. And Jesus is in the back, still asleep.

Peter shakes him awake, absolutely panicking. "Master, carest thou not that we perish?" Which is basically, "Dude, do you not care that we're about to die?"

Jesus stands up and tells the sea, "Peace, be still." And the wind just stops. Dead calm.

Then he turns to them. "Why are ye so fearful? how is it that ye have no faith?" And the disciples are just staring at each other like, "What manner of man is this, that even the wind and the sea obey him?"

So whatever storm you're in tonight? He's in the boat with you. Go read it yourself. Mark chapter four.

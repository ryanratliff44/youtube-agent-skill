The storm is so violent that professional fishermen think they are about to die. And Jesus is asleep.

Evening falls on the Sea of Galilee. Jesus tells his disciples, "Let us pass over unto the other side." They push off in a small wooden boat.

Then the wind hits. Waves break over the sides, and the boat is filling with water. Jesus is in the back of the boat, asleep on a pillow.

They shake him awake. "Master, carest thou not that we perish?"

Jesus stands, and speaks to the sea. "Peace, be still." The wind stops. And there is a great calm.

He turns to them. "Why are ye so fearful? how is it that ye have no faith?" And they ask each other, "What manner of man is this, that even the wind and the sea obey him?"

Whatever storm you're in tonight, he is in the boat with you. Read it for yourself in Mark chapter four.

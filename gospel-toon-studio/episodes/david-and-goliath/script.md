# David Beat A Giant With One Stone

**Passage:** 1 Samuel 17 (KJV quotes: v.43 "Am I a dog, that thou comest to me with staves?"; v.45 "Thou comest to me with a sword, and with a spear, and with a shield: but I come to thee in the name of the LORD of hosts.")

## Scripture vs. retelling
- "nearly ten feet tall": six cubits and a span (v.4), about 9 ft 9 in.
- "forty days": v.16. "bring his brothers some bread": v.17-18 (parched corn and ten loaves).
- "five smooth stones from a stream": v.40. "sling": v.40. "no sword": v.39-40, he put off Saul's armor and took his staff and sling.
- "young shepherd": v.33, v.42 call him a youth. His age is not stated.
- "the loudest problem" at the end is application, not scripture.

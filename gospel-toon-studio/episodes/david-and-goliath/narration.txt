Bro. A young shepherd walked onto a battlefield with no armor and no sword. To fight a giant who was nearly ten feet tall. And somehow, he's the one who wins.

Two armies are camped on opposite hills. Every morning, for forty days, a giant named Goliath walks out and dares someone to fight him. And every morning, nobody moves.

Enter David. He's just a shepherd, sent to bring his brothers some bread. He shows up, hears the giant, and can't believe nobody is doing anything about it.

King Saul tries to help. He gives David his own armor. It's so heavy David can barely walk. So he takes it off, grabs a sling, and picks up five smooth stones from a stream.

Goliath sees him coming and laughs. "Am I a dog, that thou comest to me with staves?" He feels insulted. A kid with a stick?

David answers, "Thou comest to me with a sword, and with a spear, and with a shield: but I come to thee in the name of the LORD of hosts."

Then he loads one stone, spins the sling, and lets it go. Right in the forehead. And the giant just falls over.

Moral of the story? The scariest problem in the room isn't always the strongest one. Sometimes it's just the loudest. Read it for yourself. First Samuel, chapter seventeen.

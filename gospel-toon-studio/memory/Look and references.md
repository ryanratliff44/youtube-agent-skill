## What makes the look
- **Fake, flat characters:** pure white round heads, thick black outline, dot eyes with heavy half-closed lids,
  thick brows, small mouth, thin black stick limbs, flat colored clothes with details. Big readable faces: toothy
  grins, smirks, ring eyes when shocked. They mostly face the camera.
- **Realistic world:** almost certainly a game engine (likely Unreal) with asset packs: real grass, stone, haze,
  moon, fire, torches, interiors, explosions; drone fly-overs and macro shots of props.
- One word of caption at a time in the references (Ryan now wants 1-3), white with black outline, about 70% down.
- Opens mid-action, fast hook, deadpan one-liners, steady music bed under constant narration.
- Characters are probably hand-drawn 2D art placed in the 3D scene; faces change almost every word.

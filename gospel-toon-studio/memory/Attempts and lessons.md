# Attempts and lessons
Back to [Gospel Toon Studio](Gospel%20Toon%20Studio.md)

1. **AI video (Wan 2.1 via ComfyUI)** on the RTX 4060: worked technically, looked "ugly" and "too AI".
2. **Flat sprites on AI-painted stills:** Ryan liked the idea, but it wasn't lively enough.
3. **Fast zooms and angle cuts:** too many zooms; he wants only one opening zoom.
4. **Animated 2D rigs (emotions, walking, talking):** "stick figures bouncing around"; wanted life, not mania.
5. **Code-built 3D world (three.js) with 3D characters:** "still looks 2D", then flat white-faced characters in a
   realistic-ish world (closest so far), night camp scene, faces and movement improved, but overall "nothing like
   I want".
6. **Restart (v2):** flat 2D character kit (David, Goliath, Saul, soldiers...) + AI backgrounds (SDXL) +
   narration-pinned shots. Background quality was not good enough ("the background sucks").
7. **Now: Unreal Engine** for the world (like the references), studio adds characters, voice and captions.

Lessons: approve one frame first; pin every shot to the narration; restraint beats energy; the realism of the world
is what sells the look; Ryan needs Claude to do the clicking (local session on his PC).

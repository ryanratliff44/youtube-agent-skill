# Owner preferences

## How to work with Ryan
- Not technical. One small step at a time, exact commands to paste, say what he should see. Diagrams help
  (he asked for drawn step-by-step guides several times).
- Sends phone photos of the screen; read them carefully.
- Wants Claude to do the work ("I want you to do all the creating"). Prefer scripts over asking him to click.
- Gets frustrated by versions that miss the look. Show one frame for approval before building a whole video.
- Be honest about what was and was not tested.

## Feedback that must never be repeated
- **One** quick zoom at the very start onto the main character. After that no random cuts, no zoom jumps.
  Cuts only when the story changes. Keep attention with character movement and faces.
- Characters must not jump around, flail or look frantic/distressed. Natural human movement, small gestures.
- Facial expressions must match what the narrator is saying at that moment.
- Every shot has a character in it. No empty landscapes, no random unrelated scenes (e.g. a happy beach while the
  narration is about something else).
- No scripture text on screen; he wants only the story.
- Clothes must read as robes/tunics, not "a barrel" (no vertical stripes on a capsule body).
- White faces only (no skin color, no blush). Characters simple and flat; the world around them realistic.
- Captions: the exact words of the voiceover, 1-3 words at a time, flowing fast with the narrator.
- Voice: a young, energetic "Gen Z storytime" guy (Kokoro `am_liam`); earlier tries: too fast, too AI, too jolly
  Santa.
- AI text-to-video clips looked "too AI". Code-built 3D looked "nothing like" the references.
